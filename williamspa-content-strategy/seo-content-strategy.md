## Executive Summary

**Who you are:** Florida's premier insurance claim and personal injury law firm. 30+ years, $300M+ recovered, Tampa HQ, statewide reach.

**The opportunity:** Your site has strong authority and an established index footprint, but it is significantly under-built compared to your primary competitor (Louis Law Group), which has aggressively expanded into city-specific pages, damage-type pages, and guide-style content. You have the brand credibility to outrank them — you just need the pages.

**Core SEO principle for legal:** Google ranks pages that match *exactly* what a distressed homeowner types. One page = one problem = one keyword cluster. Every gap below is a missed client.

## Page Template (For Each New Practice Area Page)

Every new page should follow this structure to rank and convert:

```
H1: [Damage Type] Insurance Claim Lawyer Florida | [City/Statewide]
Intro (150 words): What this damage type is, why Florida homeowners face it, what insurers typically do wrong
H2: Common Reasons [Damage Type] Claims Are Denied in Florida
H2: Florida Law and Your Rights (cite relevant statutes)
H2: How Williams Law Fights [Damage Type] Claims
H2: What to Do After [Damage Type] in Florida (step-by-step)
H2: Case Results (link to case-results page, mention specific outcomes)
H2: FAQ (3–5 questions with schema markup)
CTA: Free consultation form + phone number
Schema: LegalService, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList
```
